How to Pass the DELE B2 in 3 Months

Updated June 2025 · 8 min read

Three months is enough time to prepare for the DELE B2 if you're already at a solid B1 level and can dedicate 8-10 hours per week. Here's a realistic week-by-week plan that covers all four sections of the exam.

Prerequisites

This plan assumes you:

  • Can already hold conversations in Spanish on everyday topics
  • Understand the main points of clear standard speech
  • Can write simple connected text on familiar topics
  • Have at least B1 grammar (past tenses, subjunctive basics, conditionals)

Weeks 1-4: Foundation and format

The first month is about understanding the exam format and identifying your weak areas.

  • Week 1: Take a full practice exam under timed conditions. Score it honestly. Identify which sections need the most work.
  • Week 2: Focus on reading comprehension. Practice all 4 task types. Learn to identify paraphrases and distractors.
  • Week 3: Focus on listening comprehension. Practice with the 5 task types. Start daily listening habits (podcasts, news).
  • Week 4: Focus on writing. Learn the text types (formal letter, article, report, review). Write 4 practice texts.

Weeks 5-8: Skill building

The second month is about strengthening your weakest areas while maintaining the others.

  • Daily (20 min): Listen to Spanish content (podcasts, YouTube, news)
  • 3x per week (45 min): Practice your weakest section with timed exercises
  • 2x per week (40 min): Write a practice text and review it the next day
  • 1x per week (30 min): Speaking practice (language exchange, recording yourself)
  • Weekend: Take one full timed section (reading OR listening)

Grammar priorities for B2

  • Subjunctive in all its uses (wishes, doubt, emotions, temporal clauses)
  • Conditional sentences (all types)
  • Reported speech
  • Connectors and discourse markers
  • Ser vs estar in nuanced contexts

Weeks 9-12: Exam simulation

The final month is about simulating real exam conditions.

  • Week 9: Full practice exam #2. Compare with your Week 1 score. Adjust focus areas.
  • Week 10: Intensive practice on remaining weak points. Write 3-4 texts. Practice speaking tasks daily.
  • Week 11: Full practice exam #3. Focus on time management and strategy.
  • Week 12: Light review. One final practice exam. Rest the day before.

Key strategies by section

  • Reading: Don't read every word. Scan for specific information. Read questions first.
  • Listening: Use pause time to read ahead. Don't panic if you miss something — the audio plays twice.
  • Writing: Always plan before writing. Count your words. Leave 5 minutes to review.
  • Speaking: Prepare vocabulary clusters by topic. Practice thinking aloud in Spanish.

Resources

  • DELE B2 Prep app (practice exams in official format)
  • Official Instituto Cervantes sample exams (free online)
  • Spanish podcasts: Notes in Spanish, Radio Ambulante, Hoy Hablamos
  • Grammar reference: any B2-level Spanish grammar book
