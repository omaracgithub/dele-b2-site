What is the DELE B2 and What is it For?

Updated June 2025 · 6 min read

The DELE B2 (Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera, level B2) is an official certificate that proves you have an upper-intermediate level of Spanish. It is issued by Instituto Cervantes on behalf of the Spanish Ministry of Education and is recognized worldwide.

What does B2 level mean?

According to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR), B2 means you can:

  • Understand the main ideas of complex texts on both concrete and abstract topics
  • Interact with native speakers with enough fluency for regular communication
  • Produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects
  • Explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving advantages and disadvantages

Who issues the DELE B2?

The DELE diplomas are issued by Instituto Cervantes, the official institution created by the Spanish government to promote the Spanish language worldwide. The exams are designed by the University of Salamanca and administered at authorized exam centers in over 100 countries.

Does it expire?

No. Unlike many language certificates (IELTS, TOEFL), the DELE never expires. Once you pass, the diploma is valid for life. This makes it a one-time investment in your future.

Common uses for the DELE B2

Spanish citizenship

If you're applying for Spanish nationality, you need to prove A2 level. However, having a B2 demonstrates a much stronger integration and can strengthen your application overall.

University admission

Many Spanish and Latin American universities require B2 level for admission to degree programs taught in Spanish. The DELE B2 is widely accepted as proof.

Professional opportunities

Employers in Spain and Latin America often require B2 as a minimum for positions that involve communication in Spanish. It's particularly valued in education, healthcare, tourism, and international business.

Personal achievement

B2 is often considered the "breakthrough" level — the point where you can truly function independently in Spanish-speaking environments without constant support.

Exam structure at a glance

The DELE B2 consists of 4 tests grouped into 2 scoring groups:

  • Group 1: Reading (70 min, 4 tasks) + Writing (80 min, 2 tasks)
  • Group 2: Listening (40 min, 5 tasks) + Speaking (20 min + 20 min prep, 3 tasks)

You need at least 60/100 total with a minimum of 30/50 in each group to pass. The result is simply PASS or FAIL.

How to prepare

Most candidates need 3-6 months of focused preparation if they're already at a solid B1 level. The key is practicing with materials that follow the exact official format, so there are no surprises on exam day.
