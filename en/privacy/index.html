Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

DELE B2 Prep collects the minimum information necessary for the app to function:

  • Usage data: Exam progress, scores, and app preferences. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how the app is used in an aggregate and anonymous way (pages visited, session duration).
  • Subscriptions: Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data exclusively to:

  • Save your progress and preferences
  • Improve the app and fix bugs
  • Understand general usage patterns

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only third-party services we use are:

  • Google Analytics (anonymous web analytics)
  • Apple App Store (payment processing)

4. Data Storage

Your progress and app data are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain servers with personal user data.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all your data by removing the app from your device
  • Request account deletion from within the app (Profile > Delete Account)
  • Contact us for any questions about your data

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will post any changes on this page with the update date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@prepdele.com.
