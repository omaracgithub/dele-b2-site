Prepare for the DELE B2 on your phone

The DELE B2 (Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera, level B2) is an official certificate issued by Instituto Cervantes that certifies an upper-intermediate level of Spanish. It is internationally recognized and never expires, making it a permanent investment in your professional and academic future.

The exam consists of 4 tests grouped into 2 groups. Group 1 includes Reading Comprehension (70 minutes, 4 tasks) and Written Expression and Interaction (80 minutes, 2 tasks). Group 2 includes Listening Comprehension (40 minutes, 5 tasks) and Oral Expression and Interaction (20 minutes + 20 min preparation, 3 tasks). To pass, you need a minimum of 60 out of 100 points, with at least 30 points in each group.
